Packet format

Packets follow the CanSat 2026 rulebook string:

CAN-Team-XX; P-XXX; Ti-…; A-…; Pr-…; T-…; Ro-…; Pi-…; Ya-…; AX-…; AY-…; AZ-…;

Optional GP-Lat/Lon/Alt and diagnostic MODE / FAULTS / CAL / ARM tags follow the mandatory block.
